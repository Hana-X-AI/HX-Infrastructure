# Conversation Sync Policy

## Scope

This policy governs workspace-level governance decisions recorded from reviewed conversations.

## Authorized Destination

Ratified risk-acceptance decisions may be recorded only in:

```text
governance/policy/risk-acceptances.md
```

Routine actions and unresolved issues remain in:

```text
governance/logs/actions-and-issues.md
```

## Recording Rules

- Record only decisions explicitly approved by the project owner.
- Summarize the decision; do not copy conversation transcripts.
- Do not record credentials, secret values, connection details, personal data, or machine identifiers.
- Keep each acceptance scoped, time-bounded, and tied to a review or expiry trigger.
- Show the proposed diff and obtain explicit approval before changing an existing acceptance.
