Where authority sits

  • SERVER-REGISTRY.md is authoritative for assigned role, approved workload, discovery status and Phase 2 status. Where any page here disagrees with it, the registry wins.
  • servers/<host>/discovery.md records each server as found. It is never rewritten to reflect later configuration.
  • governance/actions-and-issues.md is the only routine tracking log. No separate action-item or issue documents are created.
  • Roles are assigned manually. Agents may compare capabilities and report constraints, but may not assign a role, select a workload, or choose a model.
