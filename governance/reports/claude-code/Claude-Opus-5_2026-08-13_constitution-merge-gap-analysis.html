Severity vocabulary added

governance/logs/actions-and-issues.md now carries a ## severity section defining critical / high / medium / low, required on every issue opened from 2026-08-13 and stated in bold at the start of the item text.

Two deliberate choices: closed rows were not retrofitted, because assigning a severity to a resolved issue after the fact invents evidence; and severity describes the problem, not the urgency, so a deferred critical stays critical and the deferral is recorded in the closeout column.
